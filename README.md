# Business Statistics in R

Statistical analysis project developed as part of the course  
**Statistics II** at the University of Macedonia.

## Project Overview

This project applies statistical inference techniques to a series of
business-related problems using R.

The objective was to perform the appropriate statistical analysis,
interpret the results and support the findings through statistical
visualizations.

## Statistical Analyses

### 1. Customer Service Time
- One-sample hypothesis test for a population mean
- One-sided t-test
- Calculation and interpretation of the p-value
- Visualization of the rejection region

### 2. Logistics Delivery Variability
- Estimation of population variance
- 95% confidence interval using the Chi-square distribution
- Visualization of the confidence region

### 3. Marketing Campaign Response
- Hypothesis test for a population proportion
- Two-sided z-test
- Analysis of customer response rates
- Visualization of rejection regions

### 4. Comparison of Store Sales Performance
- Comparison of two population means
- F-test for equality of variances
- 99% confidence interval for the difference between means
- Visualization of the confidence interval

### 5. Production Performance Analysis
- Comparison of production levels across three factories
- One-way ANOVA
- F-statistic and p-value analysis
- Visualization of the ANOVA rejection region

## Skills Demonstrated

- Statistical hypothesis testing
- Confidence interval estimation
- Statistical inference
- Business data interpretation
- Data visualization
- R programming
